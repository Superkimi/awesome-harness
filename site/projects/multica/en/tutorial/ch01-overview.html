01 · Architecture & ownership

SOURCE FACT

Multica models Agents, Runtimes, Tasks, Skills, and connected accounts as control-plane resources; CLI and server share the lifecycle semantics.

PLAIN LANGUAGE

Like tracing a building's pipes: entry, control, execution, and records each have a job, and edges show handoff.

IMPLICATION

Place this implementation back into the task chain and inspect input, state, effects, receipts, and recovery exits.
