Start with a concrete analogy

Context is a workbench that tidies itself: current material stays visible, old material is archived, and indexes remain recoverable.

Answer the chapter question first

What enters context, and what gets compacted or recovered? Think of Multica as an airport control tower: agents are flights, runtimes are runways, tasks are legs, and deletion must handle flights still bound to a runway.
