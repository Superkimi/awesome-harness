01 · Architecture & ownership

SOURCE FACT

SessionTurn owns a turn, Tape/Transcript/SQLite are the fact source, and agentTools, ACP, and memory provide capability and branching; the desktop UI is only one layer.

PLAIN LANGUAGE

Like tracing a building's pipes: entry, control, execution, and records each have a job, and edges show handoff.
